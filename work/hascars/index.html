HasCars

A website for a classic and exotic car storage, service and sales facility in Bucks County, Pennsylvania.

Client
HasCars
Services
Web design, Development
Live
hascars.hase.com

The brief

HasCars looks after exotic and classic cars in a climate-controlled building on PA 611, close to Philadelphia and New York. Storage, maintenance, detailing, registration and sales all happen under one roof. They needed a site that felt as cared for as the cars, and that turned visits into enquiries.

What we did

We designed and built the site in Webflow. Deep racing green, warm gold and a serif headline give it the feel of a classic car club rather than a dealership. Every service has its own page with a clear way to ask for it, the cars for sale come with the details buyers check first, and a gallery lets the collection speak for itself. The HasCars team adds new cars on their own.

Outcome

HasCars now has one place to show what they do, sell cars and take enquiries for every service, and a site the team can keep up to date themselves.
