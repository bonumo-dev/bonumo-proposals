Totemo

A playful online shop in three languages for a Czech building set made from wood and recycled plastic.

Client
Totemo
Services
Web design, Development
Year
2025
Live
totemo.eu

The brief

Totemo is a Czech building set for kids from 4 to 99, made from plywood and recycled plastic and designed with child psychologists. It has won Czech Grand Design and the Rossana Orlandi Guiltless Plastic Prize. The toy was full of play; the old website was not. Totemo needed a shop that felt as free as building with the set, and one that could sell in Czech, English and German.

What we did

We designed and built the whole site around the toys themselves. Every product page takes its colour from its photo, so the shop shifts from pink to green to blue as you browse, and each set reads like a creature of its own. A set of hand-made icons explains what makes Totemo different without a wall of text. Behind it sits a shop in three languages with stock, delivery dates and Apple Pay and Google Pay, plus pages for workshops, custom orders and the blog.

Outcome

Totemo now sells at home and abroad from one site that looks like the toy it sells: bright, a little wild and easy to use on a phone.
