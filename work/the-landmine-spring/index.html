The Landmine Spring

A new look for a Czech indie and emocore band with a long history: identity, gig posters and merch.

Client
The Landmine Spring
Services
Branding, Posters, Merch
Year
2026

The brief

The Landmine Spring have played Czech indie and emocore for years, and their look had grown up piece by piece. The band wanted one identity that feels true to where they come from, and that works on a poster in a club as well as on a shirt at the merch stand.

What we did

A wordmark with a retro, hand-set feel, and a poster system loose enough for every show: live photos in one strong colour, bold type, and the line-up and date set big. Every tour stop gets its own poster, from Cheb to Dresden.

Merch

The same identity carries over to the merch: shirts in yellow, green, white and black, each with its own print.
