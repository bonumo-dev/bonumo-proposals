Vividbooks

Front-end development of the website for Vividbooks, interactive textbooks used by more than 600 Czech schools.

Client
Vividbooks
Services
Development
Live
vividbooks.com

The brief

Vividbooks makes interactive textbooks for maths, physics, chemistry and biology that more than 600 schools use, and won Czech Grand Design for them. The new design was ready; it needed someone to build it. The site had to carry a lot: every subject with its online and printed versions, sample lessons, webinars, teacher reviews and a way to book training.

What we did

We built the site from the Vividbooks team’s design and kept every detail, down to the colour of each subject. Each subject has its own page that sets the online book next to the printed workbook, with the tools inside, sample lessons and a path to the shop. Teachers can book training with a methodologist straight from the page.

Outcome

Vividbooks got a site that is as clear and lively as its textbooks, and that walks a teacher from the first look to a lesson in class.
