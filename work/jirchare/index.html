Jircháře Grand Crew

Visuals for a natural wine pop-up at Alma Wines in Prague: a badge logo, posters and social posts.

Client
Jircháře Grand Crew
Services
Branding, Posters
Year
2026

The brief

Jircháře Grand Crew is a contemporary natural wine pop-up at Alma Wines in Prague, open on Mondays only. It needed a look that works on the door, on the wall and in the feed.

What we did

A round badge that plays with the label of a classic French bottle, set in blackletter with a nod to “appellation contrôlée”. Around it, one strong red, photos of the bar pushed into that red, and short, loud lines for posters and posts.
