IBM Research

A set of 3D illustrations for IBM Research: glass spheres and iridescent stacked forms, used across printed and digital materials.

Client
IBM Research
Services
Illustration, 3D
Year
2023

What we did

A family of 3D illustrations for IBM Research. Glass spheres that overlap and bend the light, and soft iridescent blocks that stand and stack like volumes on a shelf. One palette of deep blues, violets and greens ties them together, so every piece reads as part of the same set.

Where they live

The illustrations work on screen and in print, from presentations to printed event material such as the agenda above.
