Forbes × SAP

Digital Expedition, a Forbes Česko special with SAP that guides businesses through digital transformation as a climb to the summit.

Client
Forbes Česko
Services
Web design, Development
Year
2022
Live
digiexpedice.forbes.cz

The brief

Forbes Česko and SAP wanted to talk about digital transformation without the usual buzzwords. Their idea was an expedition: a business climbs from base camp to the summit and beyond, and at every camp Czech companies such as Košík.cz, Notino and Linet share how they got there. The special needed a home for articles, podcasts and videos that felt like the climb itself.

What we did

We designed and built the special in Webflow. The homepage reads as a route up the mountain: six camps, each with its own photo and a line that marks how high you are. Inside every camp, stories and podcasts sit side by side, and each interview gets a clean reading page with the video embedded. The Forbes team adds new stories themselves.

Outcome

A special that turns a dry topic into a journey, with one place for every story and podcast in the series.
