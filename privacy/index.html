Privacy

This notice explains what personal data Bonumo collects through this website, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Who is responsible

Needed: the data controller: company name, registration number (IČO) and registered address. Probably ELABORATE DIGITAL s.r.o. (Lukas confirms).You can reach us about anything in this notice at lukas@bonumo.com.

What we collect

  • When you book a call: your email address and, if you give it, your phone number. If you answer the optional questions, also what you are making, which way of working fits, when you need it, your company or website and your name.
  • When you email us: your message and the details in it.

We do not use analytics or advertising cookies on this website. If that changes, we will ask for your consent first and update this notice.

Why, and on what basis

We use these details only to arrange and prepare the call you asked for, and to reply to you. The basis is your consent, which you give with the checkbox on the form, and steps taken at your request before a possible contract (Article 6(1)(a) and (b) GDPR). You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to us.

How long we keep it

If we do not end up working together, we delete your details within 12 months of our last contact. If we do, we keep what the contract and accounting law require.

Who processes it for us

  • Vercel hosts this website and runs the form.
  • Resend delivers the form to our inbox, from servers in the EU (Ireland).
  • Google Workspace holds our email.
  • Adobe Fonts serves the typeface, so your browser connects to Adobe and shares your IP address with it.
  • Vercel BotID checks form submissions for automated traffic, using technical signals from your browser.

Some of these companies are based in the United States. Transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. We never sell your data or share it with anyone else.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct or delete it, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable form. Write to lukas@bonumo.com and we will answer within a month. You can also complain to the Czech data protection authority, the Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů (uoou.gov.cz).
